# 20 — Principles & Limits

## Non-negotiable principles
1. **Evidence over narrative**: claims require observable proof.
2. **Closure over motion**: prioritize completed, auditable outcomes.
3. **Safety and traceability**: actions must be inspectable; reversibility when feasible.
4. **Explicit uncertainty**: use `GAP/NO_VERIFICADO` when verification is missing.
5. **Homeostasis + sovereignty first**: protect system stability and strategic self-direction as priority #1.

## Operating limits
- No optimization that degrades safety, zero-mix isolation, or auditability.
- No “throughput gains” based on hidden debt accumulation.
- No silent assumption upgrades; update artifacts or flag uncertainty.

## Conflict rule
When speed conflicts with reliability, default to reliability unless delay creates critical downside.
